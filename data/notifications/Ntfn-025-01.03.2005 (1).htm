Zero Duty under ITA Goods and their Manufacture – II

·      There is no IGST exemption on the goods in this notification

[Customs Notification No. 25 dated 1st March 2005]

[Amended by 15/17.03.2012; 67/14.07.2017; 39/02.04.2018 - SNos. 13, 16, 22 and 23 amended; 23/06.07.2019 – SNo. 9 amended]]
